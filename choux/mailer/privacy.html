choux-mailer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who uses this app

choux-mailer is a personal tool operated by a single individual for their own use. The only Google account that authorizes it is the operator's own dedicated sender account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • gmail.send: used only to send plain-text notification emails to a fixed list of the operator's own addresses.
  • openid and email: used only to confirm that the authorized account is the intended sender account.

The app does not request, read, store or process any mailbox contents, contacts, files or other Google user data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the operator's own computer, readable only by a dedicated service account. A local log records the time, recipient and subject of each sent email; email bodies are not logged.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
